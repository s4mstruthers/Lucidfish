Train

Puzzles from your own games: every position where you went wrong. Find a better move. Puzzles you solve come back less and less often; the ones you miss come back soon, until they stick.

How it works

  • Each puzzle is a position from one of your games, just before a move the engine marked as an inaccuracy, mistake or blunder. Drag a piece to play the move you think is best.
  • Solve it on the first try and it moves up a level: you'll see it again after 1, 3, 7, 16 and then 35 days. Miss it and it drops back to the start, comes back at the end of the session, and again tomorrow.
  • After a puzzle you can read the coach's note, play out the lines, or press 🔍 Explore to try your own ideas against the engine.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser.

Cache

Lucidfish — Stockfish finds the best move; the AI coach explains why, using only facts the engine and the board have verified.

Privacy. Everything runs on this computer. With a local model (Ollama, LM Studio…) nothing leaves your machine. With a cloud provider, the evidence for each move (moves, engine lines, position facts) is sent to that provider. API keys are kept in your operating system's secure credential store and are never shown again or saved in Lucidfish's database.
Performance. Local models are processor-intensive: an 8-billion-parameter model needs about 8 GB of free RAM and is much faster with a GPU or Apple Silicon. Choose "Key moments" or a cloud provider on slower machines.

Ratings

Kept up to date by themselves: each analysed game updates the rating for its site and time control. The coach uses the rating in each game to pitch its explanations; these are only used for games without one (e.g. over the board) and the board editor. chess.com and Lichess ratings are kept apart because their scales differ.

Share this profile

Lucidfish makes one web page with this profile's analysed games, coach review and patterns. It opens in any web browser on a laptop or desktop: nothing to install, no AI or API key, and it works offline. They can browse the games and read the commentary, practise their mistakes, train on puzzles from all their games, explore positions with a built-in engine, and draw on the board.

Send a copy

Download the page and send it by email, AirDrop, WhatsApp, …

Keep it up to date in a shared folder

Choose a folder that syncs to their computer, such as a shared iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox folder. Lucidfish rewrites the page after every analysis of this profile, so opening the same file always shows the latest games.

Analysis queue

Games are analysed one at a time so Stockfish gets every core. While the coach writes up one game, the engine already works on the next, so no time is wasted. Time estimates learn from your previous analyses.
